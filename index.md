## Approach

1. **Frame the question** — What are we trying to learn or predict?
2. **Understand the data** — Shape, quality, distributions, and limits.
3. **Choose methods carefully** — Match technique to problem.
4. **Evaluate honestly** — Report what works and what doesn't.
5. **Communicate clearly** — The notebook is the deliverable.
